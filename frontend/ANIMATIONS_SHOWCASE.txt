7. EASING FUNCTIONS USED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ease-out: Smooth deceleration (used for entrances)
  ↗───    Fast start, smooth finish
  Used for: slideInDown, slideInUp, fadeIn

ease-in-out: Smooth acceleration and deceleration
  ┌─╮     Symmetric easing
  Used for: pulse-glow

linear: Constant speed (used for infinite loops)
  ───>    Steady throughout
  Used for: shimmer, rotation

cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1): Elastic bounce (result card pop)
  ↗─╮     Bouncy entrance
  ──╮
  Used for: scale-pop (gives satisfying "pop" effect)
